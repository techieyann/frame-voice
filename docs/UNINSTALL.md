# Uninstall Frame Voice

1. Open the tray icon and choose **Configure…**.
2. Select **General** and click **Uninstall…** at the bottom.
3. Choose what to retain in the confirmation, then confirm uninstall.

| Option | Keep checked to retain | Uncheck to remove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Keep current configuration** | Settings, saved API keys, and the managed custom prompt. | Those saved values and files. |
| **Keep downloaded models** | Models downloaded by Frame Voice. | Those model downloads. |

Both options are checked initially. Uncheck both for a full removal of Frame
Voice's settings, keys, models, app data, and installation backups.
Separately installed voice tools and their models are not removed.
Removing administrator-installed input rules can require your SteamOS password.
Cancelling authentication leaves the app installed.

To recover model space while keeping the app, see
[Downloaded models](SETTINGS.md#downloaded-models-and-storage).
For terminal removal or rollback, use the [developer guide](DEVELOPMENT.md).
